# 0005 — The reasoning standard: tagged claims, no divine benevolence

Date: 2026-08-15
Status: accepted (AXIOM — this is a chosen standard, not a derived claim)

## Context

Anay set the bar: everything in the lab must be truly first-principles,
almost mathematical, obvious in nature — "no divine benevolence at all."
Earlier the same day, imported best practices had been presented as settled
decisions (see D0 in MAP.md); this standard is the permanent correction.

## Decision

Every claim carries a tag: FACT (sourced), AXIOM (chosen and owned),
DERIVED (checkable chain from tagged statements), ASSUMPTION (in use,
untested, on the target list), or BET (action under uncertainty with
reversal conditions stated). Full standard in FOUNDATION.md.

Banned: appeal to practice or authority; "should work / will generalize /
scale will fix it" without a mechanism or a BET tag; treating conversational
agreement as settlement.

## Reasoning

The lab's mission is to find what the field missed by not questioning its
assumptions. A lab that reasons the way the field reasons will miss the
same things. The tagging makes every chain attackable line by line, which
is the only known way to keep hidden assumptions out.
