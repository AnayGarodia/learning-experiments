# Dossier 021 — The no-stored-data method is capped by a termination bug, not by its mechanism

Status: PRE-REGISTERED 2026-08-17. Targets decision 0011 bar (b),
continual learning. Portfolio axis: **learning**. Goal-directed: this is
the shortest identified path to retention-without-stored-data matching
retention-with-stored-data.

## The chain of evidence, all from committed records

1. **Continual learning without stored data already partly works.**
   e017's completed pilot, k=10: self-distillation on old cues (LwF)
   retains 0.563 while storing NO old examples; stored-data rehearsal
   retains 0.906. The predicted-negative control (same machinery, off-cue
   proxy inputs) retains 0.012, so the effect is real and depends on
   distilling ON the old cues.
2. **The self-distillation teacher is CORRUPTED, and the harness measured
   it.** `selfdistil_target_fidelity` — the fraction of regenerated
   teacher targets that match the true rule — reads 0.88/0.94/0.75 in the
   first steps after a skill is written and then **0.000 for every prior
   skill from k=6 onward.**
3. **The cause is the non-termination defect, and the two findings are the
   same bug.** `self_targets` generates the teacher FREELY
   (`max_new=8`, `do_sample=False`) and cuts the result at the first
   NEWLINE (`txt.strip().split("\n")[0]`). The e004g decode ruling
   established that this model emits the correct answer and then continues
   **on the same line** (`" wagon???????"`). A newline cut therefore does
   not fire, and the recorded teacher string is answer-plus-garbage. So
   `selfdistil_loss` trains the model, teacher-forced over all answer
   positions, to reproduce the garbage — and `answer_targets` takes its
   length from the corrupted string, so the scored span is wrong too.
4. **This reconciles the apparent contradiction** that skill 28 read 1.000
   teacher-forced accuracy on eval words and 0.000 target fidelity on
   train words at the same k. Both numbers are correct. Accuracy scores
   only answer positions under teacher forcing; fidelity requires the
   FREELY GENERATED string to equal the rule's output, which the
   termination bug prevents. The train/eval word split is a deliberate
   anti-leak design (`_aux_words` draws TRAIN_WORDS, retention is measured
   on EVAL_WORDS, same distribution, disjoint) and is NOT the cause.

## The claim to test

**Self-distillation's 0.563 is a floor imposed by a corrupted teacher, not
a limit of the mechanism. Fix termination and retention without stored
data should rise substantially toward the stored-data ceiling.**

The fix is small and touches the write, not the intervention: the answer's
terminator (newline, or EOS) is currently never a training target, so
nothing teaches the model to stop. Put the terminator inside the scored
span of the write.

## Rivals, pre-registered

**T1 — The teacher corruption is the binding constraint.** With
termination fixed, `selfdistil_target_fidelity` rises to approximately the
teacher-forced accuracy of each prior skill (i.e. 0.7-1.0 where the skill
is still readable, not 0.000), and selfdistil retention rises to within
0.15 of replay25's.
- IMPLICATION: continual learning without stored data is achievable at
  near-rehearsal quality in this harness, and the barrier was an
  instrumentation defect.

**T2 — Fidelity rises, retention does not.** The corrupted targets were
not what limited retention; something else does (capacity, the critical
window below, or the aux schedule).
- This is the informative negative and it is the most likely single
  outcome. It would say the mechanism, not the instrument, caps
  self-distillation.

**T3 — A CRITICAL WINDOW dominates, and the fix helps only early skills.**
Independent of the bug, the e017 records show a striking pattern: skills
whose teacher fidelity was HIGH in the first one or two steps after being
written (11, 28, 9, 24, 5, 33) survive to k=10 even while later teachers
are pure garbage, and skills whose fidelity was already ~0.0 or 0.19
immediately after writing (8, 31, 34) die and never recover. If that is
causal rather than incidental, retention is decided in a short window
after each write, and the right intervention is to spend the aux budget
THERE rather than uniformly.
- PREDICTS: after the fix, gains concentrate in the skills that currently
  die; and an aux schedule front-loaded into the first two writes after a
  skill beats a uniform schedule at equal total aux cost.
- This is a NEW mechanistic hypothesis generated by these records and it
  is worth its own arm.

**T4 — The fix breaks the write.** Adding the terminator to the scored
span changes the write objective and could reduce write strength.
MANDATORY guard: `mean_just_after` must stay within 0.05 of the current
0.927-0.937, or nothing else is interpretable.

## Design commitments

- Fix termination in the write. Re-run `selfdistil`, `replay25` (the
  stored-data ceiling) and `no_replay` (the floor) at k=10, **3 seeds**.
- **Report `selfdistil_target_fidelity` per prior per k as a primary
  outcome**, not a diagnostic. It is the mediator: the claim is
  fidelity -> retention, and both must move together for T1.
- Re-run the predicted-negative `selfdistil_offcue`. If it now retains,
  the fix has introduced a leak and the result is void.
- Free-generation accuracy must now AGREE with teacher-forced within 0.05
  (the design requirement that has been failing grid-wide). This
  experiment is also the test of whether the termination fix discharges
  that.
- Add the front-loaded-aux-schedule arm for T3 at equal total aux cost.
- Pool gate on; teacher-forced AND free-generation reported side by side;
  per-seed values; code hashed at launch; records written every step.

## Honest positioning (decision 0004 amendments 1 and 2)

The METHOD is published: LwF (Li & Hoiem 2016) / SDFT (2026), and our
write is context distillation (Snell et al. 2022). We are not proposing a
new method. What is ours here is (a) the measurement that a termination
defect caps the no-stored-data method's retention, (b) the fidelity
mediator being reported at all, and (c) the critical-window hypothesis.
A prior-art check on the critical-window claim is REQUIRED before that arm
is interpreted — front-loading rehearsal after acquisition is close to
consolidation-window findings in the memory literature and may be known.
